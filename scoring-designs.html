Baking Foundations

Scoring Designs

Scoring creates a planned opening for oven spring and can add beautiful patterns. Use a very sharp blade and make confident cuts.

Simple ear

  • Make one long, slightly angled cut along the loaf.
  • Keep the blade shallow, around 30–45 degrees, for a lifted ear.

Wheat pattern

  • Use short diagonal cuts on both sides of a central line.
  • Keep decorative cuts shallow so they do not weaken the loaf.

Before you score

  • A well-proofed loaf is easier to score when chilled.
  • Use a lame or razor carefully, and keep fingers away from the blade.
