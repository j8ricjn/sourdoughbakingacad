Starter Series · Day 6

Starter Day 6

Today, focus on timing. A strong starter rises after feeding and then slowly falls as its food is used.

Feed and mark

  • Keep 30 g starter and add 30 g water plus 30 g flour.
  • Mark the level with a band or washable marker.
  • Check the jar every few hours without stirring.

Record the rise

  • Write down when the starter doubles, if it does.
  • If it rises very slowly, keep feeding and give it more time.

Baker’s note

  • Discard any starter with fuzzy mold or pink/orange streaks; clean the jar and start again.
