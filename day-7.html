Starter Day 7

Day 7 is a checkpoint, not a deadline. Use the starter when it reliably doubles after feeding and shows a lively, bubbly structure.

Readiness check

  • It rises predictably after feeding, often doubling in about 4–8 hours in a warm room.
  • The inside looks aerated and bubbly.
  • The smell is pleasantly tangy, not rotten or moldy.

If it is not ready

  • Continue daily feeds for several more days.
  • Try some whole-wheat or rye flour to encourage activity.
  • Keep temperature and feeding amounts consistent.

Baker’s note

  • A mature starter can be stored in the fridge between regular bakes.
