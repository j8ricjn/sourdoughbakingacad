Troubleshooting Flat Bread

A loaf that spreads or bakes up flat usually gives clues in the dough texture, fermentation, shaping, or baking setup.

Common causes

  • Overproofing: dough feels weak, very bubbly, and collapses when handled.
  • Underdeveloped gluten: dough tears easily and spreads instead of holding shape.
  • Weak starter: dough barely rises during bulk fermentation.
  • Too much water for the flour: dough stays soupy and difficult to shape.

What to change next time

  • Use a starter that rises reliably after feeding.
  • Build strength with folds and watch the dough, not only the clock.
  • Shorten fermentation if the dough becomes fragile and over-expanded.
  • Reduce water slightly when trying a new flour.

Keep notes

  • Change one thing per bake so you can identify what helped.
