Sourdough Discard Pancakes

A quick way to use extra starter for tender, lightly tangy pancakes. This version uses baking powder for lift.

Ingredients

  • 120 g sourdough discard
  • 120 g milk
  • 1 egg
  • 20 g melted butter or neutral oil
  • 100 g all-purpose flour
  • 15 g sugar, 1 teaspoon baking powder, and a pinch of salt

Method

  • Whisk discard, milk, egg, and melted butter.
  • Add flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt; stir just until combined.
  • Rest 5 minutes while a lightly greased pan warms over medium heat.
  • Cook small pancakes until bubbles appear, flip, and cook until golden.

Easy variations

  • Add blueberries, cinnamon, or sliced banana.
  • If the batter is too thick, add a splash of milk.
