Starter Day 1

Today you’ll begin a living culture of flour and water. Keep the mixture clean, covered loosely, and at a comfortable room temperature.

What you need

  • 30 g whole-wheat flour
  • 30 g room-temperature water
  • A clean jar and spoon

Steps

  • Mix the flour and water in the jar until no dry spots remain.
  • Scrape down the sides and mark the mixture level with a rubber band.
  • Cover loosely and leave at 22–26°C for 24 hours.

Baker’s note

  • A few bubbles may appear, or nothing may happen yet. Both are normal on Day 1.
