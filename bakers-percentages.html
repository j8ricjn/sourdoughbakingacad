Baker’s Percentages Math

Baker’s percentages express every ingredient as a percentage of the total flour weight. This makes recipes easier to scale and compare.

The formula

  • Ingredient percentage = ingredient weight ÷ total flour weight × 100.
  • Flour is always 100%, even when you use several types of flour.

Example dough

  • 500 g flour = 100%
  • 350 g water = 70% hydration
  • 100 g active starter at 100% hydration contributes 50 g flour and 50 g water.
  • 10 g salt = 2% of flour weight.

Scale a recipe

  • Choose your total flour weight first.
  • Multiply that weight by each ingredient percentage.
  • Remember to count the flour and water inside your starter when calculating exact hydration.
