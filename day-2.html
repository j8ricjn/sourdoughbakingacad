Starter Series · Day 2

Starter Day 2

Your starter may show a few bubbles or smell like wet flour. Today, give it its first refresh and keep observing.

Feed the starter

  • Keep 30 g of the mixture and discard the rest.
  • Add 30 g water and 30 g flour.
  • Stir well, mark the level, and cover loosely.

What to watch for

  • Look for tiny bubbles and a slightly changed aroma.
  • Do not worry if it has barely risen; early activity can be inconsistent.

Baker’s note

  • Use a clean utensil each time and avoid sealing the jar airtight.
