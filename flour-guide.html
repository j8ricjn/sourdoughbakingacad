Flour Guide

Different flours absorb water and develop gluten differently. Learn what each type brings to your dough before changing a recipe.

Bread flour

  • Higher protein usually creates strong gluten and a chewy loaf.
  • A useful default for many sourdough recipes.

All-purpose flour

  • Versatile and often produces a softer crumb.
  • Water absorption varies by brand, so adjust gradually.

Whole-wheat and rye

  • Adds flavor, minerals, and fermentation activity.
  • Bran absorbs water and can make dough feel denser; rye behaves differently from wheat.

How to substitute

  • Change one variable at a time and hold back a little water at first.
  • Record the flour brand and the dough feel for your next bake.
