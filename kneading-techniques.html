Kneading Techniques

Sourdough dough often develops strength through time and gentle handling rather than long, forceful kneading.

Stretch and fold

  • With damp hands, lift one side of the dough and fold it over the center.
  • Rotate the bowl and repeat around the dough.
  • Rest 20–30 minutes between sets, as your recipe directs.

Coil fold

  • Slide both hands under the middle of the dough.
  • Lift until the ends tuck under themselves.
  • Use gentle movements to preserve the gas already inside.

When to stop

  • Dough should feel smoother, stronger, and more elastic.
  • Wet dough can feel sticky at first; avoid adding lots of extra flour.
