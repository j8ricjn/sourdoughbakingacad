Starter Series · Day 4

Starter Day 4

The starter may begin to smell tangy and show more bubbles. Keep feeding on a regular schedule and look for repeatable activity.

Feed

  • Keep 30 g starter.
  • Add 30 g water and 30 g flour, then mix thoroughly.
  • Clean the jar walls and mark the new level.

Check the environment

  • A cool room slows fermentation; a warm room speeds it up.
  • Keep the jar away from direct sun and hot appliances.

Baker’s note

  • Rise and fall between feedings are useful signs of fermentation.
