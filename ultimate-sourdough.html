Signature Recipe

The Ultimate Sourdough Loaf

A dependable country loaf with a crisp crust and an open, chewy crumb. Temperature and dough behavior matter more than the clock alone.

Ingredients

  • 500 g bread flour
  • 350 g water, plus a little extra if needed
  • 100 g active starter
  • 10 g salt

Method

  • Mix flour and water; rest 30 minutes.
  • Add starter and salt, then mix until evenly combined.
  • Bulk ferment, completing 3–4 sets of folds during the first 2 hours.
  • Let dough rise until airy and expanded, often 4–6 hours total depending on temperature.
  • Shape, place seam-side up in a floured basket, and refrigerate 8–16 hours.
  • Bake in a preheated covered Dutch oven at 230°C for 20 minutes, then uncovered for 20–25 minutes until deeply browned.

Cool before slicing

  • Let the loaf cool at least 1 hour so the crumb can set.
