Starter Series · Day 5

Starter Day 5

Your starter may be gaining strength, but readiness depends on reliable rise after feeding—not just its age.

Feed as usual

  • Keep 30 g starter and discard the rest.
  • Add 30 g water and 30 g flour.
  • Mark the level and note how long it takes to rise.

Look for strength

  • Bubbles should be visible through the mixture.
  • The aroma may be pleasantly sour, fruity, or yeasty.

Baker’s note

  • If activity is weak, continue the same routine for a few more days.
