Baking Foundations

Essential Baking Tools

You do not need a professional bakery setup to make excellent bread. Start with a few reliable tools and add more as you learn.

Start with these

  • Digital kitchen scale for accurate measurements
  • Clear jar for starter and a straight-sided container for dough
  • Mixing bowl, dough scraper, and clean towel
  • Sharp bread lame or razor for scoring
  • Dutch oven or a baking stone plus a steam-safe method

Helpful extras

  • Instant-read thermometer
  • Bench knife and proofing basket
  • Parchment paper and cooling rack

Smart buying tip

  • Spend first on a scale and a good way to create steam; many other tools have simple substitutes.
