Starter Series · Day 3

Starter Day 3

Some starters become very active around Day 3, then seem quiet again. This early burst is not a sign that the starter is ready.

Refresh

  • Discard all but 30 g starter.
  • Mix in 30 g water and 30 g flour.
  • Mark the level and leave at room temperature.

Observe

  • Note bubbles, rise, and smell before feeding.
  • A sharp or funky smell can happen during the early stages.

Baker’s note

  • Keep the feeding ratio consistent so changes are easier to track.
