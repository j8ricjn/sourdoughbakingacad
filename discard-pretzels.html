Discard Recipes

Sourdough Discard Pretzels

These soft pretzels use sourdough discard for flavor. Discard is unfed starter; it adds taste, but the recipe still needs a reliable leavening plan.

Ingredients

  • 150 g sourdough discard
  • 250 g bread or all-purpose flour
  • 120 g warm water
  • 7 g instant yeast
  • 20 g sugar and 6 g salt
  • 30 g melted butter

Method

  • Mix ingredients and knead until smooth; rise until puffy, about 60–90 minutes.
  • Divide into 6 pieces, roll into ropes, and shape pretzels.
  • Dip briefly in a baking-soda bath made with 1 litre water and 2 tablespoons baking soda brought to a gentle simmer.
  • Place on a lined tray, sprinkle with coarse salt, and bake at 220°C for 12–15 minutes.

Serve

  • Cool slightly and enjoy with mustard or your favorite dip.
